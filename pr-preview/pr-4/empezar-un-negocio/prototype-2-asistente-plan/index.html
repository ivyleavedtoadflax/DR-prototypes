Assumptions to verify

Every claim this prototype makes that isn't yet evidence-led. Test in research and stakeholder review.

About the user

  • Verify with users A personalised checklist ("solo lo que le toca") reduces overwhelm more than the full list, for a first-time founder. This is the core hypothesis vs Variant 1. (Standard 1)
  • Verify with users Founders can answer estructura (PF/EIRL/SRL) up front — or the plan must tolerate "no estoy seguro". (Standard 4)
  • Verify with users People trust a plan that hides steps as much as one that shows everything — hiding step 4/5 for a PF does not feel like something is missing. (Standard 5)

About the data

  • Verify with institución The applicability rules are right: PF skips constitution (4) and the 1% (5); TSS (8) only if hiring; the 1% is SRL-only. (Ley 479-08)
  • Verify with institución The 4 Turismo permits and their observicios links are the correct set for a tourism establishment.
  • Verify with data The three questions (estructura, hiring, sector) are enough to personalise usefully — more questions may be needed, or fewer.

About the platform

  • Verify with OGTIC GobID returns name + contact from the cédula for a persona-física founder before any company exists. (Standard 7)
  • Verify with OGTIC A saved "plan" can live in the citizen's gob.do account and be revisited.
  • Known gap The prototype shows one representative plan (SRL + hiring + tourism); it does not compute the plan from the actual answers.

About policy

  • Verify with policy Storing the founder's answers and emailing a plan is permitted under Ley 172-13 for this purpose. (Standard 11)
